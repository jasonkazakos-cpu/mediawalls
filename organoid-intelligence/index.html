About the Field

What is Organoid Intelligence?

A quick primer on one of science's most exciting new disciplines.

🧠

Brain Organoids

Lab-grown 3D clusters of human neurons derived from stem cells that self-organise into brain-like structures capable of electrical activity.

⚡

Biological Computing

Organoids can be interfaced with computers to process information, learn from feedback, and potentially solve problems — using far less energy than silicon.

🔬

Disease Modelling

OI systems provide an unprecedented platform for studying neurological disorders like Alzheimer's and autism in living human neural tissue.

🌍

Why It Matters

The human brain runs on 20 watts. Silicon AI runs on megawatts. Organoid intelligence could unlock a new paradigm of efficient, adaptive computing.
